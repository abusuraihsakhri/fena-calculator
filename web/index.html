Clinical interpretationThresholds describe classical patterns, not diagnostic certainty. FENa and FEUrea can be misleading in CKD, diuretic exposure, sepsis, glomerular disease, obstruction, and non-oliguric or evolving AKI. Interpret alongside urine microscopy, clinical course, medication history, and volume status. Do not make treatment decisions from these indices alone.
Formulae

FENa (%) = (Urine Na × Serum Cr) / (Serum Na × Urine Cr) × 100

FEUrea (%) = (Urine urea nitrogen × Serum Cr) / (BUN × Urine Cr) × 100

Urea measurements must use the same analyte definition and concentration units; creatinine values must share units.
